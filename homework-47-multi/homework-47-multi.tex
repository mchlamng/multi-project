\section*{Problem 3}

Problem: \( 5y' + y = 0 \)

\begin{equation*}
    \begin{aligned}
        & y = \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} a_{n}x^{n} \\
        & y' = \sum_{n = 1}^{\infty} na_{n}x^{n-1} \\
        & 5 \sum_{n = 1}^{\infty} na_{n}x^{n-1}
        + \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} a_{n}x^{n} = 0 \\
        & 5 \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} (n + 1)a_{n+1}x^{n}
        + \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} a_{n}x^{n} = 0 \\
        & 5 \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} [(n + 1)a_{n+1} 
        + a_{n}] x^{n} = 0.
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

Thus,

\begin{equation*}
    \begin{aligned}
        (n + 1) a_{n+1} + a_{n} = 0
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

Solving for \( a_{n+1} \):

\[
a_{n+1} = -\frac{a_{n}}{n+1}
\]

Creating a table:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
    \mathbf{n} & \mathbf{a_{n}} \\
    \hline
    0 & a_{0} \\
    [2px] \hline
    1 & -a_{0} \\
    [2px] \hline
    2 & \frac{a_{0}}{2} \\
    [2px] \hline
    3 & -\frac{a_{0}}{3 \cdot 2}
\end{array}
\]

Thus, the explicit formula is

\[
a_{n} = (-1)^{n} \frac{a_{0}}{n!}
\]

And solving for $y$ results in

\[
\begin{aligned}
    y &= \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} (-1)^{n} \frac{a_{0}}{n!} x^{n} \\
    &= a_{0} \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n} x^{n}}{n!} \\
\end{aligned}
\]

Answer: $\boxed{y = a_{0} \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n} x^{n}}{n!}}$
